# Code of Conduct

JaydenART open-source spaces should be useful, welcoming, and safe for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Give people room to ask honest questions and correct mistakes.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Protect private and personal information.

## Not Accepted

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to [GitHub@JaydenART.com](mailto:GitHub@JaydenART.com). Include links or screenshots when possible. Reports will be reviewed as privately and fairly as the circumstances allow.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may limit participation when needed to protect the community.
